# Compliance status decision rules

Apply the rules to each bidder-requirement pair after reviewing the tender clause and the bidder's searchable submission files.

| Status | Rule | Evidence needed |
|---|---|---|
| **met** | The bid makes an explicit, unqualified commitment that covers the requirement. Numeric thresholds, dates, and units must be stated where relevant. | A cited bidder passage plus the tender reference. |
| **partially met** | The bidder gives a conditional or incomplete commitment, offers a deviation, or proposes an alternative that still needs acceptance. | Cite the limitation or alternative and record the unresolved question. |
| **not met** | The bidder explicitly refuses, excludes, or contradicts the requirement without a usable compliant alternative. | Cite the refusal or exclusion and record the clarification question. |
| **not evidenced** | No relevant evidence is found, the passage is too vague to support a decision, or two bid passages conflict. Ambiguity is not treated as compliance. | List every bid document searched. If passages conflict, cite both. |
| **not applicable** | The bidder gives a verifiable scope reason showing the requirement falls outside its assigned work. A bare “N/A” is insufficient. | Cite the scope statement and include its justification. |

## Cross-cutting controls

- A mandatory requirement cannot be marked **met** without a supporting bidder passage.
- Irrelevant brochures or generic catalog sheets do not count as supporting evidence.
- If one passage commits and another excludes or limits the same requirement, record `not evidenced`, flag `contradiction`, and ask the bidder to resolve it.
- Record the tender quotation and source page for every pair. For any substantive bid finding, retain the bidder quotation, file, and page.
- Keep missing evidence separate from an explicit refusal: absence maps to **not evidenced**; a clear refusal maps to **not met**.
- Keep mandatory gaps visible when status is `partially met`, `not met`, or `not evidenced`.
- Clarification rows always include the bidder name and stable requirement ID.
