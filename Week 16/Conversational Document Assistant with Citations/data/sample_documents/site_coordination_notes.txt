Cubic Engineering Consultancy Site Coordination Notes

During project coordination, engineers often ask follow-up questions about previous answers. The assistant should maintain conversation history so that follow-up questions can be understood in context.

A realistic scenario is an engineer asking about required approval documents, then asking which team is responsible for the next step.

The system should improve retrieval quality by cleaning text, splitting documents into meaningful chunks, and ranking the most relevant chunks before answering.

The assistant must avoid unsupported conclusions. When the answer cannot be found in the documents, it should respond with an insufficient information message.
